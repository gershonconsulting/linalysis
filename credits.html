Pending invitations — last 90 days
How LinkedIn enforces these
✉️ InMail credits
Granted every billing month. Unused credits roll over up to 3 months of allowance. A reply within 90 days refunds the credit.
🤝 30,000 connections
LinkedIn's hard ceiling. At the limit every new connection needs a removal first — which is why new and removed are tracked separately.
📨 Weekly invitations
LinkedIn limits invitations per rolling week and does not publish the exact figure. Many unanswered or "I don't know this person" invitations lower it; withdrawing old pending ones helps.
