Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

We keep cookies to a minimum. No advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking.

What cookies we use

CookiePurposeDurationType
linalysis_session Keeps you logged in between visits. Required to use the service. 30 days Essential
linalysis_csrf Protects form submissions from cross-site request forgery attacks. Session only Essential
linalysis_prefs Remembers your settings (date range, chart mode, monthly/yearly billing toggle). 1 year Functional
__cf_bm Cloudflare bot management — protects the site from automated abuse. 30 minutes Essential
cf_clearance Cloudflare verification cookie after you pass a challenge (if any). 30 days Essential

What we don't use

  • Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any other analytics SDK on this marketing site.
  • Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or other ad-retargeting pixels.
  • Third-party ad networks or data brokers.

Managing cookies

Your browser lets you block or delete cookies any time. Blocking essential cookies will break your login and settings — blocking functional cookies just resets your preferences. See:

  • Chrome — privacy & cookie settings
  • Firefox
  • Safari

Chrome extension

The Linalysis Chrome extension reads your LinkedIn session cookie (li_at) only to validate it via our CookieVerify API. We never copy or store this cookie. The extension runs locally in your browser.
